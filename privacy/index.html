SCENTRECON

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

ScentRecon has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your phone

Saved properties, stand locations, trails, deer sign and alert times are stored only on your device. They are not backed up to our servers; we have none. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app sends to work

  • Address searches go to Mapbox to find the place.
  • The area you are viewing (a map point) goes to Mapbox for satellite imagery, Open-Meteo for the weather forecast, Amazon Web Services for elevation tiles, and the USDA Forest Service for tree canopy.

These providers receive the request and your IP address as part of normal internet traffic, under their own privacy policies:

  • Mapbox: mapbox.com/legal/privacy
  • Open-Meteo: open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • AWS: aws.amazon.com/privacy
  • USDA Forest Service: usda.gov/privacy-policy

Location

With your permission, the app reads your location only while it is open, to center the map and for Follow me. It is not stored, tracked in the background or sent anywhere except as the map area described above.

Notifications

Switch alerts are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them leaves the device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts to confirm what you bought. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

ScentRecon is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
