SCENTRECON

Support

ScentRecon shows where your scent goes on real terrain, hour by hour, so you can pick the stand and the time the thermals work for you.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: bltrippdev@gmail.com. Include your phone model and the place you were looking at if something looks wrong.

Purchases

ScentRecon Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Accuracy

ScentRecon is a planning tool. It models thermals from terrain, sun and forecast wind. Always confirm the real wind on stand with a puffer bottle or milkweed.
